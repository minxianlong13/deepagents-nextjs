## Objective

Enable “Per stay” tax configuration in Passkey so event owners can accurately model one-time, per-reservation charges (e.g., environmental taxes, housekeeping fees, city surcharges) and surface them transparently in the attendee booking site and related flows, in line with:

-   California SB 478 / AB 537 and similar state rules
    
-   FTC “junk fee” pricing transparency regulations
    

  

## Problem Statement

Today, Passkey taxes/fees at event level are effectively per night only. Customers who need per reservation / per stay behavior are forced to:

-   Hack these fees as artificially small per-night taxes, or
    
-   Mention them in free-text descriptions (hotel description, policies), which do not participate in calculations or booking site breakdowns.
    

This causes:

-   Incorrect math for one-time fees (e.g., €5 per stay being charged €5 × nights if modeled per night).
    
-   Non-compliant / opaque display for mandatory per-reservation fees under FTC and California transparency rules (fees not clearly itemized or misrepresented in nightly prices).
    

Concrete examples:

-   ConferenceDirect asked for a control that supports “per night or per reservation” frequency for fees/taxes; product confirmed this maps to per-stay support in Phase 2.
    
-   Visit Athens CVB needs to disclose a mandatory housekeeping fee per reservation (hotel charges it) in Passkey’s tax/fee area, not just in description text.
    

  

## Goals:

-   Let Passkey users configure Per stay frequency for applicable taxes/fees.
    
-   Model the changes for processing fees, deposit fees and cancellations fees
-   Ensure correct calculation and display across hotel list, room list, extend stay, calendar, reservation summary, and confirmation.
    
-   Avoid breaking existing per-night tax behavior or current Pricing Transparency Phase 1/2 implementations
    

## Background

-   California SB 478 / AB 537 and FTC rules require that mandatory fees (resort, service, admin, processing fees, etc.) be clearly disclosed upfront as part of the effective total price, not added later as surprises.
    
-   Pricing Transparency Phase 1 & 2 focus on:
    
    -   Marking taxes/fees as “Include in Displayed Rate” vs “Exclude from Displayed Rate”.
        
    -   Adding Rate Details modals and consistent breakdown across hotel list, room list, extend stay, and summary pages.
        

-   Examples -
    
    #### 1. **Per Night Tax**
    
    -   **Lodging Tax / Occupancy Tax**
        
        -   Example: A hotel in New York City may charge a lodging tax of $3.50 per night.
            
        -   A 5-night stay would incur $17.50 in taxes.
            
        -   Purpose: Covers daily wear, city services, tourism infrastructure.
            
    
    #### 2. **Per Stay Tax**
    
    -   Tourism Fee / City Surcharge
        
        -   Example: In Amsterdam, hotels charge a one-time €5 environmental tax per stay.
            
        -   Flat fee regardless of 1 night or 7 nights.
            
        -   Purpose: To fund environmental or cultural initiatives.
            
    -   Cleaning Fee (often in vacation rentals)
        
        -   Example: A $25 per stay cleaning fee on Airbnb or vacation properties.
            
        -   Not linked to the duration but to post-stay service costs.
            

  

### Questions:

-   Per Stay Meaning:

Per stay tax is applied per room type, per hotel.  
For each room type booked, a single per stay amount is charged for the entire stay, regardless of the number of nights. If multiple room types are booked, the per stay amount is charged once for each room type. This also applies when room types are split across different hotels: the per stay amount is applied separately for each room type in each hotel.

Examples (assuming per stay tax = $10):

-   One hotel, one room type
    -   Hotel A, Room Type 1  
        Stay: 5 nights  
        Per stay tax: $10 total (not 5 × $10)
-   One hotel, multiple room types
    -   Hotel A, Room Type 1 (3 nights)  
        Hotel A, Room Type 2 (2 nights)  
        Per stay tax:  
        Room Type 1: $10  
        Room Type 2: $10  
        Total per stay tax = $20
-   Multiple hotels, multiple room types
    -   Hotel A, Room Type 1 (2 nights)  
        Hotel B, Room Type 3 (4 nights)  
        Per stay tax:  
        Hotel A – Room Type 1: $10  
        Hotel B – Room Type 3: $10  
        Total per stay tax = $20

-   Can I apply 2 different tax types for the same hotel ( Tax 1 as "per stay" and Tax 2 as "per night"): Yes
-   For multi‑hotel in the same event, can hotel have different tax setup (per‑stay vs others per‑night): Yes
-   Percentage will be applicable for per stay tax

### Frequency Dropdown

-   Add a **Frequency** field for each event-level tax/fee row in **Event → Details → Taxes & Fees**.
-   Allowed values:
    -   **Per night** (default, current behavior).
    -   **Per stay** (new).
-   For **existing** tax rows, migration sets **Frequency = Per night** to preserve behavior.

  

### 1.2 Supported Combinations

**Frequency = Per night** (existing behavior) supports:

-   Fixed amount per night.
-   Amount per night.
-   % of total amount including taxes.
-   % of total amount excluding taxes.
-   First night room rate including taxes (applied and divided per night as today).
-   First night room rate excluding taxes (applied and divided per night as today).

**Frequency = Per stay** supports:

-   Fixed amount per stay (e.g., flat €5 per stay).
-   % of total amount excluding taxes per stay.
-   % of total amount including taxes per stay.
-   Models that use **first night rate** as the base, applied **once per reservation** (per stay), aligned with the Pricing Transparency matrix.

  

  

## Open Questions

1.  **Scope of frequency support per tax model**
    
    -   Are there any tax models where **Per stay** should be explicitly disabled (e.g., amount per night)?
        
2.  **Per-stay application unit**
    
    -   Athens CVB use case is clearly **per reservation per hotel**. Do we also need a configuration for **“per room per stay”** in a future phase?
        
3.  **Inclusion with “Base Rates are Tax and Fee Inclusive”**
    
    -   Should per-stay taxes ever be auto-baked into base rates when that toggle is on, or always shown as separate line items even if “Include in Displayed Rate” is checked?
        
4.  **Reverse rate calculation**
    
    -   Current Slack discussion indicates **per-stay taxes are excluded from reverse rate calculators** due to ambiguity when distributing across nights.
        
    -   Do we need to update reverse rate logic for per-stay taxes now, or explicitly document that they are **not** included in reverse computations (and why)?
        
5.  **Multi-room semantics for per-stay tax**
    
    -   For a reservation with multiple rooms, do some customers expect per-stay tax **once per room** vs once per reservation? If yes, should that be a separate configuration dimension (Frequency: per stay per reservation vs per stay per room) in a later phase?
        

## Risks

-   **Regulatory misinterpretation:**
    
    -   Incorrect mapping of per-stay vs per-night behavior could still violate or confuse compliance expectations if not carefully aligned with SB 478 / FTC guidance.
        
-   **Complex UX:**
    
    -   Overloading the tax screen with too many options (per night vs per stay vs include/exclude) can confuse non-technical users.
        
-   **Calculation edge cases:**
    
    -   Multi-night, suppressed nights, and mid-stay modifications can produce confusing per-night breakdowns if per-stay amounts are reallocated incorrectly.
        

## Success Metrics

-   At least **X% of new California-compliant events** using per-stay taxes where appropriate within 3–6 months of release.
    
-   **0 critical** bugs related to tax total mis-calculation in production after 2 release cycles.
    
-   Demonstrable **reduction in support tickets** specifically about “per reservation/per stay” housekeeping/city/environmental fees (baseline vs post-release).
    
-   Positive validation from early customers (e.g., **ConferenceDirect**, **Visit Athens CVB**) that per-stay needs are fully addressed.
