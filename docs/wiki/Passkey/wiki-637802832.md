## Overview

This wiki discusses issues with the inline banners on Event Inventory page related to reservation delivery.

## Background Reading

From our old reference guide - [https://info.passkey.com/help/en\_us/modern\_eventsetup\_content\_event\_summary\_content.htm](https://info.passkey.com/help/en_us/modern_eventsetup_content_event_summary_content.htm)

Section of note is  Adding or Managing Hotel Reservation Notification Emails

## Problem

Currently on CW events, we show several types of banners on the inventory screen.

There are 4 varieties shown that depend on the specific hotel configuration:

-   Warning - Reservation email not set
-   General - Update reservation email
-   General - Hotel is 2-way
-   General - Hotel is 1-way (not shown below)

  

IMPORTANT: An event may be created without entering hotel notification email addresses. It is recommended that email addresses be added when the event is created to ensure all hotels receive the proper notifications.

NOTE: Any changes to the Reservation Notification email address in the hotel profile will not automatically update addresses in an event. _Additionally, updates to email addresses at the event level, will not update the Hotel Profile.  ( I think this last sentence is not correct and it will update at hotel profile)  
OT: Changes on the Hotel level will propagate down to the Event **if** Event level e-mail address remains untouched and defaults from the Hotel level. Once a user modifies an Event level address, subsequent changes on the Hotel level will no longer propagate down to that Event._

TIP: The Hotel Welcome Email may be generated straight from the Email Notification pop-up by clicking the Send icon. The campaign sent will be specific to the type of hotel (integrated or non) and a new campaign for the property will be created and stored in the Communications area of the event.

  

## Reservation Contacts

The relevant email always comes from the Hotel Profile from the field "Reservation Contact Email".

This email is copied down to the event and is seen on the Inventory screen, and also on the Delivery Status screen.

  

## Banners

This section discusses the variety of banners that are currently shown.

These banners are ONLY shown for organizer events - e.g. owned by a plain org or sister property organizer.

They are NOT shown for a Hotel Direct (HD) event.

### Email Not Set

Red banner is shown when email is not set.

**Warning! Hotel reservation email address must be set. Please click here to set email address.**

This means: No email address currently exists in the Hotel's profile and should be added.

NOTE: Setting this email on the Event does actually update the Hotel profile.

User can set the email via this dialog.

  

NOTE: This email address is used for the "Hotel Welcome Emails".

### Email Update

**Reservation email notifications will be sent to this hotel. Please click here to update email address.**

What this means: Clicking this message displays the email addresses as defined in the Hotel Profile (_at the time the event was created_). They may be modified for this event.

  

NOTE: This email address is used for the "Hotel Welcome Emails".   we probably need to clarify the wording and buttons here.    I guess Send will save this also.  In this case it should trigger the "non-integrated hotel welcome email" IF there is 1+ of these, and 1+ are ON.  We are not precise if you have multiple of these, which will be sent.  That last phrase is added only if 1+ are ON, so is not always shown.

### Hotel Is 2-Way Integrated

**Hotel is GroupLink integrated for 2-Way reservation transfer to the hotel system**.

What this means: Hotel is integrated with Passkey and has electronic delivery of reservations from Passkey to their property. Additionally, reservations made on-property are delivered to Passkey. Hotel receives alerts regarding unprocessed reservations from GroupLink.
