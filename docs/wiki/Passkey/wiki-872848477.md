### What is SLB 478? 

SB 478 (California) is a California law that went into effect on July 1, 2024, designed to combat drip pricing — where consumers see a base price initially, but the final total increases during checkout with added fees

#### Key Requirements of SB 478:

1.  Total price must be advertised upfront
    
    -   The full, all-in price — including mandatory fees (like service, resort, admin, processing fees) — must be clearly disclosed at the beginning of the booking journey.
        
2.  No hidden or surprise fees
    
    -   Any required charge must be part of the initial price shown to the guest.
        
3.  Optional fees (e.g., parking, pet fees if not applicable to all) can still be excluded — _but must be clearly itemized and disclosed_.
    

There are multiple rules that apply where we might need separate handling. Since the processing fee could be of multiple types

-   Credit Card Processing Fee (non mandatory) could be left as it can be paid in cash also
-   Service Fee / Admin Fee
-   Online Convenience Fee

  
Regulatory Implications (e.g., California SB 478)  
If any of the above fees are mandatory and not optional, they:

-   Must be included in the advertised total price shown to the consumer upfront.
-   Cannot be added later during checkout or disclosed in fine print.
