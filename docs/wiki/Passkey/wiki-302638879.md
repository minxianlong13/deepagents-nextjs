In other words failure of callback should not prevent create/modify/cancel reservation.</span></li><li><span style="color: rgb(0,51,102);">Allow parallel execution of callback, as separate step in passkey reservation-saga, parallel to sending acknowledgment. Call to callback should happen as one of the last steps of reservation-saga parallel with acknowledgement. This way will allow us to not perform roll back.</span></li></ul><h1><span style="color: rgb(0,51,102);">Passkey-reservation functional gaps</span></h1><p><span style="color: rgb(0,51,102);">Need to add the following:</span></p><ul><li><span style="color: rgb(0,51,102);">GET .../passkey-reservation/v1/reservations/callback → get all necessary information to do a callback.
