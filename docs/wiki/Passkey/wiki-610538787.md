## Overview

Document to address growing challenges with hotels setting up GroupLink for events being managed by EC customers (Citywide).

## Background

Dedicated slack channel - [https://cvent.slack.com/archives/C05PP9F9KTJ](https://cvent.slack.com/archives/C05PP9F9KTJ)

Figma [notes](https://www.figma.com/file/KiRRXLeNZIxmirdezKyvTc/GroupLink-Simplification-Kickoff?type=design&node-id=402-1085&mode=design&t=kWljMX8FUcAstGnp-0).

## Problem

We are seeing a sharp rise in reports of friction from our EC Passkey customers that revolve around hotels (both integrated and non-integrated) refusing to accept reservations via Passkey functionality for a variety of reasons and this is resulting in high levels of friction for our EC customers and driving substantial risks for adoption and renewals.

This friction is being identified as an urgent problem that we need to work together to address as quickly as possible. My current view is that this challenge is both multi-faceted and complex. As such, I expect addressing these challenges will require changes on multiple fronts and a series of incremental product enhancements to start shift this trend in the right direction.

  

Need to - Reduce friction to deliver reservations from Passkey to Hotels

  

Some problems:

-   Hotel Communications (e.g. ensure great templates for all Smart Alerts and Emails, and system generated emails.  For campaigns/alerts, clarify the settings and add some where needed)
-   Passkey sold only to hotels historically
-   Opportunity to sell Passkey directly to Planners
-   Hotels and Planners have difficulty integrating the Planner booking site & reservations into their Hotel reservation system
-   Hotel Staff / Users may not have a lot of experience in the hotel business and don’t have time to learn new process
-   Hotels are offering competing products that also give planners insight into hotel reservations
-   Hotels don’t have a good way of knowing that a planner has created an event with their hotel. They don’t trust the event that’s being built in Passkey.
-   Group Link Licensed Hotels vs. Non-Licensed Hotels
-   A better way for a planner to know if a specific hotel is licensed

  

## Key Definitions

GroupLink Integration - Exchange of guest reservations data between Passkey and Hotel system

Licensed - You can create and own the event in Passkey

## Current Process Buckets

-   Planner Owned event w/ hotels non GroupLink integrated & non - license
-   Planner Owned event w/ hotels that are GroupLink integrated but not Passkey licensed
-   Planner Owned event w/ hotels that are GroupLink integrated with a Passkey license

## Personas and Use Cases

Who will be the primary users of this?

-   Hotel Staff
-   Event Planners

### Ideal Outcomes

What are the primary users’ ideal outcomes?

-   Hotel Staff - Understands why GroupLink is important. Increase confirmation and opportunities post reservation dates  
    Accepting Planner invitation for their events to be easily integrated into their hotel reservation system
-   Event Planners - The planner doesn’t have to do anything different regardless of which “Bucket” the hotel is in, i.e. Integration / License
