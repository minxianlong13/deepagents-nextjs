<p><ac:structured-macro ac:name="toc" ac:schema-version="1" ac:macro-id="3b06e5fa-7717-4d7a-8bf3-a8143f2bc058" /></p><h2>Developer registration</h2><ac:structured-macro ac:name="warning" ac:schema-version="1" ac:macro-id="638d79c7-8bf8-4c31-a391-2bb779626701"><ac:rich-text-body><p>Facebook requires a user that will be the administrator of the app and will own it. Before creating an app, please check that you are using the right Facebook account to log in. </p></ac:rich-text-body></ac:structured-macro><p>Every Facebook app has to be created under a registered developer in Facebook account.</p><p>All steps are listed and well documented - <a href="https://developers.facebook.com/docs/development/register">https://developers.facebook.com/docs/development/register</a> but for reference:</p><h3>Step 1: Start the registration process</h3><p>While logged into your Facebook account, go to the<span> </span><a style="text-decoration: none;" href="https://developers.facebook.com/">Facebook for Developers</a><span> </span>website and click<span> </span><strong>Get Started</strong>.</p><h3>Step 2: Agree to our Terms and Policies</h3><p>Click<span> </span><strong>Next</strong><span> </span>to agree to our<span> </span><a style="text-decoration: none;" href="https://developers.facebook.com/terms">Platform Terms</a><span> </span>and<span> </span><a style="text-decoration: none;" href="https://developers.facebook.com/devpolicy">Developer Policies</a>.</p><h3>Step 3: Verify your account</h3><p>We will send a confirmation code to the phone number and email address that you provide in order to confirm that you have access to them. Your number and email will be used for important developer notifications of any changes that may impact to your app.</p><h3>Step 4: Select your occupation</h3><p>Select an occupation that most closely describes what you do for a living.</p><h3>Next Steps</h3><p>Now that you have registered you can use the App Dashboard to<span> </span><a href="https://developers.facebook.com/docs/development/create-an-app" style="text-decoration: none;">create your first app</a>.</p><h2>App creation</h2><p>In order to log in with Facebook social login feature, we have to create and register an application in Facebook development account that we just created.</p><p>All steps are listed and well documented - <a href="https://developers.facebook.com/docs/development/create-an-app">https://developers.facebook.com/docs/development/create-an-app</a> but for reference:</p><h3>Step 1: Start the app creation process</h3><p>If you just came from the registration flow, click the<span> </span><strong>Create First App</strong><span> </span>button.</p><h3>Step 2: Choose an app type</h3><p>Your app type determines which products and APIs are available to your app. If this is your first time creating an app and you are just exploring the app creation flow, choose the<span> </span><strong>None</strong><span> </span>option. Later, when you are more familiar with our products and APIs, refer to our<span> </span><a style="text-decoration: none;" href="https://developers.facebook.com/docs/development/create-an-app/app-dashboard/app-types">app types</a><span> </span>document to determine which app type is best suited for your app, then create a new app and choose an appropriate type.</p><p><strong>DEV NOTE:</strong> Passkey Autoblock project should have <strong>Consumer </strong>(consumer includes Facebook login feature)<strong>.</strong></p><h3>Step 3: Set your app name and email</h3><p>Enter the name of your app and an email address where we can send you any important developer notifications. The email address can be different from the email address associated with your Facebook account, just make sure it's valid and that you monitor it, since all important developer notifications will be sent there.</p><p>You can also add a<span> </span><a href="https://www.facebook.com/business/help/113163272211510" style="text-decoration: none;">Facebook Business Manager account</a><span> </span>if you have one, then click<span> </span><strong>Create App</strong>.</p><p><strong>DEV NOTE: </strong>Passkey Autoblock app should have &lt;TBD&gt; name and &lt;TBD&gt; email. (&lt;TBD&gt; needs stackeholders input)</p><p>Once you have completed the app creation flow your app will be loaded in the App Dashboard.</p><h3>Next Steps</h3><p>Now that you have created your app you can start<span> </span><a href="https://developers.facebook.com/docs/development/build-and-test" style="text-decoration: none;">building and testing</a>. You can also explore the<span> </span><a href="https://developers.facebook.com/docs/development/create-an-app/app-dashboard" style="text-decoration: none;">App Dashboard</a><span> </span>and provide additional information about your app, but it's not necessary to start building. You can always return the dashboard and adjust settings as they are needed.</p><h2>Business Verification</h2><p>At the early phase of the development of Passkey Autoblock, the app <strong>doesn't</strong> need to be verified as a business app and connected to any business-related API.
