If there are invalid or missing billing fields in a dropped off rooming list, then they get completely blocked from being able to make the credit card association valid because they no longer have access to re-enter all the card details from the original upload in order to correct missing or incorrect billing info.</p><p>Multiple customers reported the issues they were facing last year after the upgrade, and RLM was prioritized at the time to fix.  </p><p><strong>Where we are today:</strong> RLM supports billing-only fixes, single and bulk, through a workaround we built in Passkey (Option B discussed later). The other channels do not support billing-only updates yet. In those channels a user must enter a whole new card and billing to change anything.</p><p><strong>Preferred direction: That the payment wallet team supports billing-only updates natively, so every channel shares one UX and one source of truth (see Option A below).</strong></p><h2>Background Reading</h2><table><thead class=""><tr class=""><th>Reference</th><th>What it covers</th><th>Impact and status</th></tr></thead><tbody class=""><tr class=""><td><strong><a href="https://jira.cvent.com/browse/GROUP-114027">GROUP-114027</a></strong><br />Credit Card Information Wiped When Entering Billing Address in RLM</td><td>Entering or fixing a billing address in RLM wipes the credit card data on every reservation that is touched. In short, GroupLink is <strong>likely not affected by the wallet limitation, but this is unconfirmed.</strong></p><ul><li><strong>Same card:</strong> Mapping rule 360 (LMS, vendor 450) preserves the stored card number when the card type and last 4 digits match the inbound token.</li><li><strong>New card:</strong> Under the same rule, if the card type or last 4 digits differ, the inbound token is accepted and stored.</li><li><strong>Not the wallet:</strong> Inbound cards are vendor tokens. For LMS they are stored as a custom payment (RESCUSTOMPAYMENT and EXTRESERVATIONDATA), not through the PBB wallet.</li><li><strong>Billing address gap:</strong> The inbound payment card carries no billing address for HMS or LMS. LMS fills it from the guest profile address, and HMS likely needs the same. Mapping rule 1100 (IHG) blocks guest address updates on inbound modifications, which is a guest address rule rather than a billing rule.</li><li><strong>Not documented:</strong> The field-level modify logic for an inbound reservation is marked &quot;to be reviewed later&quot; in the inbound documentation. The investigation notes also appear to predate the wallet.</li></ul><p>A GroupLink developer should confirm this, or it should be tested in a lower environment, before we call it done.</p><p>Sources: <a href="https://wiki.cvent.com/pages/viewpage.action?pageId=483309179">Inbound Reservation and Status transfers</a>, <a href="https://wiki.cvent.com/pages/viewpage.action?pageId=65656412">Cvent Passkey GroupLink Mapping Rule Definitions</a>, <a href="https://wiki.cvent.com/pages/viewpage.action?pageId=533002227">Dev notes (GROUP-89760)</a>.</p><h2>Open Questions</h2><table><thead><tr><th>Reporter</th><th>Question</th><th>Outcome</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td><br /></td><td><strong>GroupLink:</strong> When a hotel pushes a modification with a new card, or the same card with new billing, does the stored card survive?
