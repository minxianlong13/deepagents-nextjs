## **Key Considerations**

* * *

### **1\. Software Architecture Perspective**

#### **Advantages of Storing Raw Files**

1.  **Data Reusability:**
    
    -   Useful for debugging, audits, or recalculating mappings with updated business logic.
    
      
    
2.  **Decoupling Processing Pipelines:**
    
    -   Files stored in a repository (e.g., Amazon S3)
        -   Enable asynchronous and parallel processing by various services. and achieve milestone. 
    -   Facilitates modular architecture,
        -   Where raw files can serve as a canonical source of truth.
3.  **Version Control:** Are we thinking to introduce new usecase that user will upload multiple files? and compine them. or change them later on before creation ?
    
    -   Raw file storage supports versioning, enabling comparison between the original and transformed data during iterative development .
    
      
    

#### **Disadvantages of Storing Raw Files**

1.  **Increased Storage Complexity:**
    
    -   Managing metadata for stored files and ensuring proper indexing adds complexity to system architecture.
    -   Risk of redundant storage if files are unnecessarily re-uploaded.
2.  **Scalability Concerns:**
    
    -   Continuous accumulation of raw files can lead to scalability challenges, especially if file sizes are large or growth is unbounded.

* * *

### **2\. Security Perspective**

#### **Advantages of Storing Raw Files**

1.  **Compliance with Data Integrity Requirements:**
    
    -   Storing raw files ensures that the original data is preserved for audits or regulatory compliance.
2.  **Traceability:**
    
    -   Enables tracking the history of data transformations and decisions, providing a clear chain of custody for sensitive information.

#### **Disadvantages of Storing Raw Files**

1.  **Data Sensitivity and Compliance Risks:**
    
    -   Raw files may contain Personally Identifiable Information (PII) or Payment Card Industry (PCI) data, exposing the system to strict compliance requirements (e.g., GDPR, HIPAA, PCI DSS).
    -   Improper handling of these files can result in significant legal and financial penalties.
2.  **Attack Surface Expansion:**
    
    -   Storing raw files increases the system's attack surface, as these files may become a target for data breaches.
3.  **Retention and Deletion Policies:**
    
    -   Securely managing the lifecycle of raw files (e.g., implementing TTL, deletion after processing) adds operational overhead.

* * *

### **3\. AWS Cost Perspective**

#### **Advantages of Storing Raw Files**

1.  **Cost-Effective Storage Options:**
    
    -   AWS S3 provides cost-effective storage solutions, with options such as Standard-IA (Infrequent Access) or Glacier for archival storage.
2.  **Avoiding Reprocessing Costs:**
    
    -   By storing raw files, the system avoids incurring additional compute costs from re-uploading and reprocessing data repeatedly.

#### **Disadvantages of Storing Raw Files**

1.  **Storage Costs:**
    
    -   Continuous storage of large files or a high volume of files may result in significant monthly costs, particularly if frequent retrieval is required.
2.  **Transfer Costs:**
    
    -   Moving raw files between storage and processing services (e.g., S3 to Lambda) incurs additional AWS data transfer costs.
3.  **Data Lifecycle Management Costs:**
    
    -   Implementing automated cleanup or archival processes increases AWS management overhead and complexity.

* * *

### **4\. Technology Perspective**

#### **Challenges with Storing Raw Files**

1.  **Data Format Compatibility:**
    
    -   Supporting various file formats (CSV, XLS, PDF, etc.) requires robust and extensible processing pipelines.
    -   Technologies such as **AWS Textract** for parsing and extracting structured data.
2.  **Encryption and Security:**
    
    -   Encryption (e.g., AWS KMS) must be applied to stored files to ensure data security.
3.  **Error Handling and Recovery:**
    
    -   Storing raw files requires mechanisms for detecting corrupted or incomplete uploads..

#### **Advantages of Not Storing Raw Files**

1.  **Real-Time Processing:**
    
    -   Systems can process uploaded files in real time and discard raw files post-processing, reducing storage needs.
    -   This aligns well with stateless, serverless architectures.
2.  **Reduced Compliance Burden:**
    
    -   By not storing raw files, the system avoids retaining sensitive data, reducing exposure to compliance risks.
3.  **Simpler Architecture:**
    
    -   Eliminating the need to store raw files reduces the complexity of infrastructure and dependency management.

* * *

### **Final Recommendation**

The decision to store raw files depends on balancing the trade-offs between architectural needs, security, compliance, cost, and operational simplicity.

#### **Store Raw Files If:**

-   Regulatory compliance or auditability requires retaining the original data.
-   The workflow needs to support reprocessing, versioning, or debugging.
-   Storage costs are manageable, and sufficient encryption/security measures are in place.

#### **Do Not Store Raw Files If:**

-   Data processing can occur in real-time without requiring reprocessing.
-   The raw files contain highly sensitive information that cannot be adequately secured.
-   Cost considerations or architectural simplicity are top priorities.
