## Goals

-   Provide messaging capabilities between event owners and event stakeholders to improve communication and Planner Portal stickiness
-   One step closer to Migrate Roche off the old Sub-block Dashboard (Note - Delivering on this scoped functionality alone is not the only dependency to migrate Roche's portal off of sub-block dashboard)

## Background and strategic fit

A rudimentary form of messaging called the "Bulletin Board" exists in the old Sub-block Dashboard and is currently only used by Roche. This feature was never built into the second generation Planner Portal. Now that we are sunsetting that second generation in favor of the third generation (Project Odyssey) it's time to build in messaging. This will be one more step in the right direction of being able to sunset Sub-block Dashboard. It is also a feature that other customers have been requesting to improve their communication. It also offers a wealth of analytic possibilities to help us understand how our customers use Passkey and potential pain points.
