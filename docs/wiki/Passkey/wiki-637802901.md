## Overview

This wiki discusses Smart Alerts/Updates within a CW event and the lack of visibility into activity.

## Background Reading

Community article - [https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/Managing-SmartAlerts-in-Passkey?is\_article=true&lang=en\_US&searchFor=smartalerts%20citywide%20events](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/Managing-SmartAlerts-in-Passkey?is_article=true&lang=en_US&searchFor=smartalerts%20citywide%20events)

## Problem

Alerts and updates can be set up to go out to a specific set of known users at organization and/or hotels that are participating in the events.

They can also go to additional recipients who are not Passkey users.

However, there's no "report" or other indication of activity, only that this alert/update exists and is turned ON.

Possible Solution

There is no tracking of alerts/updates in the product, so there's no way to know how many were sent, opened, read, by whom, what date, etc.
