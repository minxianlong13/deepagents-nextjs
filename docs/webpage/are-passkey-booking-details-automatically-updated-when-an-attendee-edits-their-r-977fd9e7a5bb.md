# Are Passkey booking details automatically updated when an attendee edits their registration?

FAQEvent ManagementPasskey

When an attendee changes their registration details for a Cvent event [integrated with Passkey](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/Setting-Up-the-Passkey-Integration-in-your-Cvent-Event), the corresponding Passkey booking details will not be automatically updated to reflect the changes on the registration side. The attendee will need to [access their Passkey reservation](https://support.cvent.com/Passkey/s/communityarticle/Modifying-or-Cancelling-a-Reservation-on-the-Passkey-Booking-Website) and make the necessary changes.
