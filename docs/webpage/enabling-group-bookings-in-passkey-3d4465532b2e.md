# Enabling Group Bookings in Passkey

InstructionsPasskey

Follow the steps below to enable [group bookings](https://support.cvent.com/Passkey/s/communityarticle/Using-Group-Bookings-in-Passkey) at the [profile](#PK_lib_GroupBooking), [bundle](#PK_bundle_GroupBooking), or [event](#PK_event_GroupBooking) level.   
   
  
In the Hotel or Organization Profile

**1 Access the organization or hotel defaults**. Log in to [Passkey](https://manage.passkey.com/), hover over Manage and click **Organizer Setup** or **Hotel Setup**. From the left-hand navigation, click **Defaults**.

**2 Enable group bookings.** Scroll down to the Reservation Defaults section and check the box next to "Enable Group Booking workflow when." Enter the minimum number of rooms that need to be booked in order to activate this workflow.

 

![User-added image](https://support.cvent.com/servlet/rtaImage?eid=ka02G000000LzdU&feoid=00N2G00000CxUw1&refid=0EM2G000000okLQ)

  
Click **Save**. From this point on, any bundles or events that are created by the hotel or organization will have the group booking workflow enabled by default.  
  
At the Bundle Level

**1 Access the Bundles page**. Log in to [Passkey](https://manage.passkey.com/), hover over Manage and click **Organizer Setup** or **Hotel Setup**. From the left-hand navigation, click **Bundles**.

**2 Enable group bookings.** Click the name of the bundle you want to edit and scroll down to the "Specify Additional Booking Options" section. Check the box next to "Enable Group Booking workflow when." Enter the minimum number of rooms that need to be booked in order to activate this workflow.

 

![User-added image](https://support.cvent.com/servlet/rtaImage?eid=ka02G000000LzdU&feoid=00N2G00000CxUw1&refid=0EM2G000000okLa)

  
Click **Save**.  
  
At the Event Level

**1 Access the Details page**. Locate your event on the Passkey Home page and click its name to access it. From the left-hand navigation, click **Details**, then **Details** again.

**2 Enable group bookings.** Scroll down to the "Additional Booking Options" section and check the box next to "Enable Group Booking workflow when."  Enter the minimum number of rooms that need to be booked in order to activate this workflow.

 

![User-added image](https://support.cvent.com/servlet/rtaImage?eid=ka02G000000LzdU&feoid=00N2G00000CxUw1&refid=0EM2G000000okM4)

  
Click **Save**.

**3 Configure your attendee types**. From the left-hand navigation, click **Advanced Options**, then **Attendee Types**. Click the name of the attendee type you need and select the number of reservations the attendee should be allowed to book in one session.  
 

**NOTE:** The reservations per session must be equal to or higher than the minimum number of rooms needed to trigger the group booking workflow.

![attendee type.png](https://support.cvent.com/servlet/rtaImage?eid=ka02G000000LzdU&feoid=00N2G00000CxUw1&refid=0EM2G000002iAb4)

  
Click **Save**.

**4 Set up master acknowledgement emails**. From the left-hand navigation, click **Communications**, then **Group Campaigns & Acknowledgements**. Scroll down to the Master Acknowledgements section. Click **Add** to start [creating acknowledgement emails](https://support.cvent.com/Passkey/s/communityarticle/Managing-Passkey-Acknowledgement-Emails) specifically addressed to the group booking contact.

This email includes the master acknowledgement number, a unique code that ties the individual reservations together. The group booking contact can use this master acknowledgement number to manage the group booking as a whole.

Each reservation within a group booking will still have its own acknowledgement number so it can be managed individually as well.
