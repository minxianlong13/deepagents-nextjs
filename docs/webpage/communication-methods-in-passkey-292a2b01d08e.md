# Communication Methods in Passkey

Whether you want to provide your guests with important information about their reservations or notify your assigned event planners about specific event milestones, Passkey offers a wide range of methods for communicating efficiently. Below, you can find the various communication types you can use or customize based on your needs and the target audience.

Group Campaigns

[Campaign email templates](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/Managing-Campaign-Emails) are generally used to send marketing messages and notifications to your event guests and planners. They include:

-   **Pre-Arrival** - Can be sent to reservation holders a few days before they check in. It usually includes reservation details and useful information about the destination and the upcoming event. If you are a Passkey-licensed event organizer, you can use the pre-arrival campaign to send hotel confirmation numbers to guests after the [Reservation Access Date](https://support.cvent.com/Passkey/s/communityarticle/Key-Dates-and-Times-in-Passkey-Events#PK_Hotel_inv_dates).
-   **Post-Stay** - Can be sent after your guests check out. You can use it to thank them for staying at your hotel, send a satisfaction survey, or offer a discount for their next stay.
-   **Upsell** - Can be sent to all reservation holders or only to those associated with specific attendee or room types. You can use it to promote room upgrades, extended stays, hotel amenities, or local attractions.
-   **Announce Event** - Can be sent to a custom list of recipients and is generally used to provide more information about the event, the venue, and participating hotels.
-   **Quick Notify** - Can be used to inform about last-minute changes, special services or offers at the event venue or hotel, or more details about the cancellation policy. It can be sent to all reservation holders, attendees who haven't yet booked a room, attendees with waitlisted reservations, or to a custom list of recipients.
-   **Registered/Not Booked** - Can be used in [RegLink-enabled](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/Enabling-RegLink-in-a-Passkey-Event) events to notify successfully registered attendees that they have not yet booked accommodations. This campaign email helps prevent attendees from booking rooms outside the contracted block.
-   **Planner Email** - Can be sent to the assigned event planners to provide them with important information such as how to access the [Planner Portal](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/Passkey-Planner-Portal-Overview) and manage reservations, drop room lists, or track pickup.
-   **Booking Contact Quick Notify** - This campaign template only shows up when [group bookings are enabled](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/Enabling-Group-Bookings-in-Passkey#PK_lib_GroupBooking), and it can be sent to group booking contacts to inform them about last-minute changes or important event details.
-   **Room Block Requested** - Can be sent to a custom list of recipients when creating a [public block request](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/Creating-Public-Block-Requests-in-Citywide-Events#Automatic) where inventory is automatically allocated from available Free-Sell.
-   **Room Block Approved** - Is sent to sub-block group contacts whose sub-block requests were [approved](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/Approving-a-Public-Block-Request).
-   **Sub-block deadline reminder** - Can be sent to an event's sub-block group contact to remind them of the reservation deadline date.

Acknowledgements

[Acknowledgement emails](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/Managing-Passkey-Acknowledgement-Emails) act as reservation confirmations that can be sent to your guests. They include:

-   **New** - This notification can be sent to guests when they successfully book rooms or when a booking is made on their behalf. It includes the reservation acknowledgement number and reservation details such as guest and room information, rates, and the cancellation policy.
-   **Modify** - This notification can be sent to guests when they make changes to their existing reservation or when their reservation is modified on their behalf.
-   **Cancel** - This notification can be sent to guests when they cancel their booking or have it cancelled on their behalf.

**NOTE:** If the [group booking workflow is enabled](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/Enabling-Group-Bookings-in-Passkey) in an event, you can also send master acknowledgements to the group booking contact who creates new group reservations, modifies existing ones, or cancels a group booking.

Notifications

These notification templates can be used in [sub-block requests](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/Managing-Sub-Block-Requests-in-Passkey). They include:

-   **Sub-Block Request Open** - This notification is specific to private block requests and can be sent to sub-block group contacts to let them know that the sub-block request form is now available for them to fill in and submit.
-   **Deadline** - This notification is specific to private block requests and can be sent to sub-block group contacts to remind them that the request deadline is coming up soon.
-   **Reminder** - This notification is specific to private block requests and can be sent to sub-block group contacts to remind them about important milestones related to the sub-block request.
-   **Assignment** - This notification is specific to private block requests and informs sub-block group contacts that inventory was allocated for their request.
-   **Request Allocation** - This notification is specific to public block requests and informs sub-block group contacts of their room block allocation. It includes details such as number of allocated rooms, cancellation policies, and links to the planner portal. It also allows contacts to accept the allocated room block.

Hotel Communications

The hotel communication emails can only be [set up](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/Managing-Campaign-Emails) and sent by Passkey-licensed organizers to hotels participating in a [Citywide event](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/Passkey-Glossary#:~:text=Citywide%20Event). They include:

-   **Integrated Hotel Reservation Notification** - Once the [reservation access date](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/Key-Dates-and-Times-in-Passkey-Events#PK_Hotel_inv_dates) has been reached, this communication can be sent to the [contacts](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/Managing-Hotel-Contacts-in-Passkey) of a participating hotel integrated with Passkey. It provides information about reservations that haven't been transferred because the [GroupLink setup](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/Setting-Up-GroupLink-for-a-Passkey-Event) is incomplete, and reservations that haven't been successfully processed.
-   **Non-Integrated Hotel Reservation Notification** - Once the [reservation access date](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/Key-Dates-and-Times-in-Passkey-Events#PK_Hotel_inv_dates) has been reached, this communication can be sent to the [contacts](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/Managing-Hotel-Contacts-in-Passkey) of a participating hotel not yet integrated with Passkey. It provides a reservation summary, including the number of reservations that haven't yet been processed, and advice on how to use the [RezHUB RoomList Download feature](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/Using-the-RoomList-Download-Feature).
-   **Hotel Collaboration** - Can be used to inform participating hotels about last-minute changes or additional event details.

SmartAlerts

[SmartAlerts](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/Managing-SmartAlerts-in-Passkey) are scheduled notifications that can be sent to specific recipients such as event planners, hotel contacts, account users, or various stakeholders who do not have a Passkey user account. There are three types of SmartAlerts you can send:

-   **Updates** - Contain critical event information, such as block, pickup, and reservation details. You can schedule them to be sent at regular intervals, such as daily, weekly, or monthly.
-   **Alerts** - Are triggered when certain inventory conditions or event milestones are met, such as an event block reaching 80% capacity. These milestones can be customized for each event.
-   **Hotel instruction emails** - If you're organizing a [Citywide event](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/Passkey-Glossary#:~:text=Citywide%20event) in Passkey, each hotel participating in your event will receive an automated instruction email guiding them through the steps they need to perform to ensure Passkey reservations are successfully delivered to their central reservation or property management system. [Reference this article](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/Configuring-Hotel-Instruction-Emails) to learn how to specify when the instruction email should be sent out for each hotel in your event and who should receive it.
