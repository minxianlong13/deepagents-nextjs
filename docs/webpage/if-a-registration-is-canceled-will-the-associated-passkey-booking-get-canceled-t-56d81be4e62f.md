# If a registration is canceled, will the associated Passkey booking get canceled too?

FAQEvent ManagementPasskey

When an attendee cancels their registration to a Cvent event [integrated with Passkey](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/Setting-Up-the-Passkey-Integration-in-Registration), their Passkey booking will not be automatically canceled. However, the attendee will be prompted to cancel or modify their Passkey booking during the registration cancellation process.
