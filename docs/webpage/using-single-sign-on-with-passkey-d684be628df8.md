# Using Single Sign-On with Passkey

InstructionsPasskey

Single Sign-On (SSO) is an authentication service that allows you to use the same set of login details to access several applications. To have SSO authentication enabled for your organization, contact your Cvent account manager. Once SSO authentication has been activated in your account, follow the steps below to log in using this service.  
  
If You Have One Passkey Account

**1 Specify which organization you are part of.** Go to [manage.passkey.com/?auth\_type=sso](https://manage.passkey.com/?auth_type=sso) and enter the name of your organization.

**NOTE:** Not sure what name you need to enter? Contact your Passkey administrator to find out.

 ![Overview of the SSO Login screen, with the name of an organization entered in the Organization field.](https://support.cvent.com/servlet/rtaImage?eid=ka0Ui0000002i09&feoid=00N2G00000CxUw1&refid=0EMUi0000054iNl)

Click **Log In**. You will be redirected to your organization's internal platform login page.

**2 Enter your corporate credentials.** Log in with your corporate username and password. If this is the first time you are going through the SSO process, you will next be asked to enter your Passkey credentials.

From this point on, you will no longer need to use your Passkey credentials when you want to access your Passkey account.

If You Have Multiple Passkey Accounts

**1 Specify which organization you are part of.** Go to [manage.passkey.com/?auth\_type=sso](https://manage.passkey.com/?auth_type=sso) and click **Have multiple Passkey accounts? Tell us which one to use**.

![The link "Have multiple Passkey accounts? Tell us which one to use" is highlighted on the SSO Login screen.](https://support.cvent.com/servlet/rtaImage?eid=ka0Ui0000002i09&feoid=00N2G00000CxUw1&refid=0EMUi0000054i4P)

Enter your organization's name and the Passkey username you want to authenticate with on this occasion.  
 

Click **Log in**. You will be redirected to your organization's internal platform login page.

**2 Enter your corporate credentials.** Log in with your corporate username and password. If this is the first time you are going through the SSO process, you will next be asked to enter your Passkey credentials for the account you want to access.

From this point on, you will no longer need to use your Passkey credentials when you want to access this specific Passkey account.
