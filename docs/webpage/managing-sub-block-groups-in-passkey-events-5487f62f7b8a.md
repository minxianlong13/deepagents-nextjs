# Managing Sub-Block Groups in Passkey Events

With sub-block groups, you can allow specific event planners to manage reservations or request inventory for specific attendee types in your Passkey event. Moreover, if you have defined [deposit, processing, and cancellation fees](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/Setting-Up-Deposit-Processing-and-Cancellation-Fees) in your event, you can [report on the sub-block groups' commerce activity](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/How-do-I-track-transaction-activity-in-Passkey). Follow the steps below to learn how to [set up a sub-block group](#SBGroupAdd), [add contacts](#Planner) to it, [edit a sub-block group](#SBGroupEdit), or [remove one](#SBGroupDel). 

Creating a Sub-Block Group

**1 Access the Sub-Block Groups page.** Begin by selecting your Passkey event. From the Event navigation, click **Sub-Block Group Management**, then **Sub-Block Groups**.

**2 Add a sub-block group from the library.** Click **Add group** in the top-right, then **Add from sub-block group library**. Click **Next**.

Select an existing [library sub-block group](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/Managing-Sub-Block-Groups-in-Passkey-Libraries), then click **Next**.

Select the desired attendee types. If necessary, define additional attendee types by clicking **Create attendee type**.

**NOTE:** Once you add an attendee type to a sub-block group, you cannot associate that attendee type to other sub-block groups.

Click **Add sub-block group**.

**Or, create a new sub-block group.** Click **Add group** in the top-right, then **Create new sub-block group**. Click **Next**.

Give it a name. If necessary, check the box next to **Add to sub-block group library**, but keep in mind you will need to manage its contacts at the [library level](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/Managing-Sub-Block-Groups-in-Passkey-Libraries).

Select the desired attendee types. If necessary, define additional attendee types by clicking **Create attendee type**.

**NOTE:** Once you add an attendee type to a sub-block group, you cannot associate that attendee type to other sub-block groups.

Click **Add sub-block group**.

Adding Contacts to a Sub-Block Group

**1 Access the Sub-Block Groups page.** Begin by selecting your Passkey event. From the Event navigation, click **Sub-Block Group Management**, then **Sub-Block Groups**.

**2 Add contacts to a sub-block group.** Locate the group you need, then click its name to access it. The Overview tab opens by default. Click the tab to the right, **Details**.

Scroll down to the Sub-Block Contacts section and click **Add contact**.

Enter the contact information such as the email address, name, organization, phone number, and locale.

**NOTE:** If the contact already exists in the system, their details will be automatically populated as soon as you enter the email address.

Click **Next**.

Choose whether or not a welcome email should be sent to the contact as soon as you finish adding them to the sub-block group. If you choose to send an email immediately, review its content and, if necessary, customize it per your needs. You can change the email subject, From name, and content, and you can select a [Reply-to email](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/Setting-Up-Reply-To-Email-Addresses) or a [planner email template](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/Managing-Campaign-Emails).

Click **Add contact and send**.

**NOTE:** If you choose not to send an email, click **Add contact** instead.

You can add as many contacts as you want to a sub-block group. Moreover, you can add the same contact to multiple sub-block groups.

If you added several contacts to a group, you can designate one of them as the primary contact by clicking the **Select primary contact** icon next to their name. The primary contact will receive any sub-block notifications sent by the event organizer.

If you want to see what the sub-block group contact sees on the Planner Portal, click the **More** icon to the right, then select **View Planner Portal as this contact**.

To manage what sub-block group contacts can see and do, [configure the sub-block group planner portal settings](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/Managing-the-Sub-Block-Group-Settings).

Editing Sub-Block Groups

**1 Access the Sub-Block Groups page.** Begin by selecting your Passkey event. From the Event navigation, click **Sub-Block Group Management**, then **Sub-Block Groups**.

**2 Edit the sub-block group.** Locate the group you need to edit, then click its name to access it. The Overview tab opens by default. Click the tab to the right, **Details**.

Here you can update the group name and manage the associated attendee types.

In the Sub-Block Contacts section, click the **More** icon to the right of a contact to access additional options such as editing the contact's details, removing the contact from the sub-block group, sending an invitation email, or [viewing the Planner Portal as the contact](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/Viewing-the-Planner-Portal-as-a-Planner-or-Sub-Block-Group-Contact).

By default, all sub-block groups you add to an event will have the [same settings](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/Managing-the-Sub-Block-Group-Settings). If you want to customize the settings for this specific group, click the tab to the right, **Planner Portal Settings**.

[Make the changes you need](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/Managing-the-Sub-Block-Group-Settings#SubBlockGroup), then click **Save**.

Deleting Sub-Block Groups

**1 Access the Sub-Block Groups page.** Begin by selecting your Passkey event. From the Event navigation, click **Sub-Block Group Management**, then **Sub-Block Groups**.

**2 Delete the sub-block group.** Locate the group you want to remove, then click the **More** icon to its right. Select **Delete**.

Click **Yes, delete** to confirm.
