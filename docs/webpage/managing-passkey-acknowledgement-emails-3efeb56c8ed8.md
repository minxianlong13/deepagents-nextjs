# Managing Passkey Acknowledgement Emails

In Passkey, you can set up acknowledgement emails to be sent out whenever attendees make, modify, or cancel a booking. These acknowledgement emails will be sent to the primary guest as well as any additional room guests on a reservation provided their email addresses were entered during the booking process. If [you enabled group bookings](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/Enabling-Group-Bookings-in-Passkey), you can also set up master acknowledgement emails to go out to the booking contact whenever they make, modify, or cancel a group booking.

Follow the steps below to learn how to [create](#PK_comm_ack_create), [edit](#PK_comm_ack_edit), [copy](#PK_Comm_Ack_Copy), or [remove](#PK_Comm_Ack_Del) an acknowledgement email.

Creating an Acknowledgement Email

**1 Access your event acknowledgement emails.** Begin by selecting your Passkey event. From the left-hand navigation, click **Communications**, then **Group Campaigns & Acknowledgements**. Scroll down to the Acknowledgements section.

**Or, access the acknowledgements list in your library.** If you want to create acknowledgement emails to be used in bundles, begin by hovering over Manage at the top of the page, then click **Hotel Setup** or **Organizer Setup**. From the left-hand navigation, click **Libraries**, then **Communications**.

**NOTE:** Changes you make at the library level will not affect existing events.

**2 Create an acknowledgement email**. Click **Add** next to the acknowledgement email type you wish to create.

Enter a name for the new acknowledgement email.

If you are creating an acknowledgement email at the event level, you can select which attendee types will receive it. Click **Select Attendee Types**. Check the boxes next to the ones you want and click **Select**.

**NOTE:** An attendee type can be assigned to only one acknowledgement email within an event. If an attendee type has already been assigned to an acknowledgement email, it will no longer be available for selection when creating or configuring another acknowledgement email.

**3 Set the email languages.** In the Languages section, switch on the locales for which you wish to [create translated versions](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/Translating-Communications-in-Passkey) of the acknowledgement email.

**4 Manage the email content.** Click **Layout** to select how your email content should be compartmentalized.

Enter the email subject and what name should appear in the From field.

If you have set up any [Reply-to email addresses](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/Setting-Up-Reply-To-Email-Addresses) in your hotel or organization profile, you can use the Reply-To Email field to select one.

Use the available formatting tools at the top of the message box to customize the email body per your needs and [add images](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/How-do-I-add-images-to-Passkey-emails). You can also use [email tags](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/Adding-Tags-to-a-Passkey-Campaign-or-Acknowledgement-Email) to populate specific information based on who the recipient is.

To see how the new acknowledgement email will look like to its recipients, click **Send test message** and enter your email address, then click **Send**.

**NOTE:** Any information included in a test message is generic and not based on your Passkey event or account data.

Click **Save** when you're done.

**5 Enable the acknowledgement email**. Locate the newly created acknowledgement and enable it by switching the toggle next to it to **ON**.

Editing an Acknowledgement Email

**1 Access your event acknowledgement emails.** Begin by selecting your Passkey event. From the left-hand navigation, click **Communications**, then **Group Campaigns & Acknowledgements**. Scroll down to the Acknowledgements section.

**Or, access the acknowledgements list in your library.** If you want to customize acknowledgement emails to be used in bundles, hover over Manage at the top of the page and click **Hotel Setup** or **Organizer Setup**. From the left-hand navigation, click **Libraries**, then **Communications**.

**NOTE:** Changes you make at the library level will not affect existing events.

**2 Edit an existing acknowledgement email**. Click the ![Edit](https://support.cvent.com/servlet/rtaImage?eid=ka0Ui000000MA0j&feoid=00N2G00000CxUw1&refid=0EMUi000004QLMf) icon next to the acknowledgement email you want to start editing it.

Make the necessary edits to its content, then click **Save**.

**NOTE:** In a hotel library, if an acknowledgement email displays the ![Corporate Standard Template](https://support.cvent.com/servlet/rtaImage?eid=ka0Ui000000MA0j&feoid=00N2G00000CxUw1&refid=0EM2G000001Z1ju) icon, it cannot be edited or deleted as it was created by a parent organization such as a chain or brand.

Copying an Acknowledgement Email

**1 Access your event acknowledgement emails.** Begin by selecting your Passkey event. From the left-hand navigation, click **Communications**, then **Group Campaigns & Acknowledgements**. Scroll down to the Acknowledgements section.

**Or, access the acknowledgements list in your library.** If you want to manage acknowledgement emails to be used in bundles, hover over Manage at the top of the page and click **Hotel Setup** or **Organizer Setup**. From the left-hand navigation, click **Libraries**, then **Communications**.

**NOTE:** Changes you make at the library level will not affect existing events.

**2 Copy the acknowledgement email.** Locate the email you need and click the ![Copy](https://support.cvent.com/servlet/rtaImage?eid=ka0Ui000000MA0j&feoid=00N2G00000CxUw1&refid=0EM2G000001Z2MX) icon to its right.

Set a name for the new acknowledgement, then make any other changes you need.

Click **Save** when you're done.

Deleting an Acknowledgement Email

**1 Access your event acknowledgement emails.** Begin by selecting your Passkey event. From the left-hand navigation, click **Communications**, then **Group Campaigns & Acknowledgements**. Scroll down to the Acknowledgements section.

**Or, access the acknowledgements list in your library.** If you want to manage acknowledgement emails to be used in bundles, hover over Manage at the top of the page and click **Hotel Setup** or **Organizer Setup**. From the left-hand navigation, click **Libraries**, then **Communications**.

**2 Delete an existing acknowledgement email.** Locate the acknowledgement email you need and click the ![Delete](https://support.cvent.com/servlet/rtaImage?eid=ka0Ui000000MA0j&feoid=00N2G00000CxUw1&refid=0EM2G000001Z1jz) icon to its right. Click **Yes** to confirm.

**NOTE:** In a hotel library, if an acknowledgement email displays the ![Corporate Standard Template](https://support.cvent.com/servlet/rtaImage?eid=ka0Ui000000MA0j&feoid=00N2G00000CxUw1&refid=0EM2G000001Z1ju) icon, it cannot be edited or deleted as it was created by a parent organization such as a chain or brand.

If you can't delete an acknowledgement email or if you want to temporarily prevent it from being sent or added to a bundle, you can disable it instead by switching the toggle next to it to **OFF**.
