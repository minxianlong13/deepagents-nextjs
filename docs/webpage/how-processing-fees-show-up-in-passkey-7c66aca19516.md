# How Processing Fees Show Up in Passkey

If you have [set up processing fees](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/Setting-Up-Deposit-Processing-and-Cancellation-Fees) in your Citywide Passkey event, these will display in multiple places, such as:

-   [your event's booking website](#Website)
-   [the Call Center form](#CallCenter)
-   [communication emails](#Emails)

Reference the sections below to find out where processing fee information is displayed in each of the locations listed above.

The Booking Website

When an attendee accesses the booking website and selects their stay dates, the processing fee will be calculated into the nightly room rate displayed across the pages of the booking website. For any [processing fee model](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/What-types-of-fees-are-available-in-Passkey-Commerce) apart from "Amount per night," the total fee amount will be divided across the number of selected nights. This means that the displayed nightly rate will fluctuate depending on the number of nights selected.

Information about the processing fee will be displayed on the following pages of the booking website:

-   **The Hotels page** - The processing fee model and disclosure message will be displayed in the Policies section when an attendee clicks **View hotel details** in a hotel listing.
-   **The Rooms page** - The total processing fee amount will be displayed in the Included Fees section when an attendee clicks **View rate details** in a room listing.
-   **The Reservation Summary section** - The total processing fee amount will be displayed under Included Fees in the Reservation Summary section that appears to right of the booking website after an attendee selects a room. The processing fee amount will be calculated into the Subtotal amount.
-   **The Guest Information page** - The processing fee model and disclosure message will be displayed in the Policies section.
-   **The Summary page** - The total processing fee amount will be displayed under Included Fees in the Reservation Charges section, and the processing fee model and disclosure message will be displayed under Reservation Policies. The processing fee will be calculated into the Subtotal and Grand Total amounts.
-   **The Guest Deck page** - The total processing fee amount and disclosure message will be displayed on the reservation confirmation page, under Included Fees, when the guest expands the booked room details. The processing fee will be calculated into the Subtotal and Grand Total amounts.

The Call Center Form

The processing fee model and disclosure message will be displayed in the right-hand frame of a [Call Center Form](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/Understanding-the-Call-Center-Reservation-Form-in-Passkey) when the call center user selects a hotel, or when they click **Policy/Rates** at the bottom of the Call Center Form for a reservation.

Communication Emails

The processing fee name, model, and disclosure message will be displayed in communication emails such as [acknowledgements](https://support.cvent.com/Passkey/s/communityarticle/Managing-Passkey-Acknowledgement-Emails) and [campaigns](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/Managing-Campaign-Emails) for reservation holders as long as you have [inserted the ${Reservation.ProcessingFee} and {Reservation.ProcessingFeeDisclosure} tags](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/Adding-Tags-to-a-Passkey-Campaign-or-Acknowledgement-Email).[](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/The-Complete-Passkey-Booking-Site-Experience)
