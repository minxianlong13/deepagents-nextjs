# Managing Campaign Emails in Passkey

Group campaigns are used to send emails to your event participants. Passkey offers a wide variety of [campaign email templates](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/Communication-Methods-in-Passkey#PK_CampaignTypes) to fit your needs, whether you are a hotel user or a Citywide event organizer. You can create as many campaign emails as you want with each template. Follow the steps below to learn how to [create](#PK_Campaign_Create), [edit](#PK_CampaignEdit), or [remove](#PK_CampaignDel) a campaign.

Creating Campaigns

**1 Access your event campaigns.** Begin by selecting your Passkey event. From the Event navigation, click **Communications**, then **Group Campaigns & Acknowledgements**. The available campaign emails are listed in the Group Campaigns section.

**Or, access the campaigns in your account library.** If you want to create or customize campaign emails to be used in bundles or future events, hover over Manage in the top navigation, then click **Hotel Setup** or **Organizer Setup**. From the left-hand navigation, click **Libraries**, then **Communications**.

**NOTE:** Changes you make at the library level will not affect existing events.

**2 Create a new campaign email.** Click **Add** next to the campaign type you need.

If you are creating the campaign at the event level, click **New Campaign** in the next prompt.

Enter a name for your new campaign email.

**3 Specify when the campaign should be sent.** In the Timing section you can set the email to be sent a specific number of days before or after an event milestone date. You can also set a specific time of day when the campaign email should be sent. The date milestones vary depending on the campaign type you create, and can be the [event start, reservation access, cutoff, or the event end date](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/Key-Dates-and-Times-in-Passkey-Events).

**NOTE:** If you are creating a campaign at the event level, you can also select a custom date.

**4 Specify who will receive the campaign.** Depending on the campaign type, you can set the recipients to be:

-   **All Reservation Guests.** Each guest on a reservation will receive the campaign.
-   **Reservation Primary guest only.** Only the first guest on the reservation will receive the campaign.
-   **Import List.** The Announce Event and Quick Notify campaign types allow importing a list of email addresses from a spreadsheet.
-   **Prior Event.** The Announce Event and Quick Notify campaign types allow using email addresses from a previous event.
-   **Registered attendees without a reservation.** If your event is [Reglink-enabled](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/Enabling-RegLink-in-a-Passkey-Event), the Quick Notify and Registered/Not Booked campaign types can be configured to send only to registered event attendees who haven't completed their room booking.
-   **All registered attendees.** If your event is [Reglink-enabled](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/Enabling-RegLink-in-a-Passkey-Event), the Registered/Not Booked campaign type can be configured to send to all attendees who registered to the event, whether they booked rooms or not.
-   **Wait List.** The Quick Notify campaign type can be configured to send only to guests who have waitlisted reservations.
-   **In-house reservation guests.** The Quick Notify campaign type can be configured to send only to guests who have checked in.
-   **Attendee or Room Types.** The Quick Notify campaign type can be configured to send only to guests associated with a specific attendee or room type.
-   **Event planners.** The Planner Email campaign type can only be sent to the planners that were assigned to the event.

For [Hotel Communication emails](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/Communication-Methods-in-Passkey#PK_HotelComm), the recipients can be set as all the hotels participating in a Citywide event, only the Passkey integrated hotels, or only the hotels with no Passkey license or integration. You can also designate specific hotels by clicking **Select Hotels**, then checking the boxes next to the hotel names you want. Click **Select** when you're done. If you check the box next to "Send copy to organizer," the hotel communication email will also be sent to the email addresses added to the Reservation Notification Email field in the [organization defaults](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/Hotel-and-Organization-Profile-Defaults).

Check the box next to "Respect the Opt-in setting" to avoid contacting guests who have requested not to receive promotional emails.

If the [group booking workflow is enabled](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/Enabling-Group-Bookings-in-Passkey) in your event, you can check the box next to "Exclude Guests with Booking Contact" to avoid contacting guests with reservations that are part of a group booking.

**NOTE:** You can add up to 2,000 recipients. To send the campaign to more than 2,000 people, create additional campaign emails for the next set of recipients.

**5 Set the campaign email language.** In the Languages section, switch on the languages for which you wish to [create translated versions](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/Translating-Communications-in-Passkey) of the campaign email.

The default event language will always be enabled.

**6 Customize the email content.** If necessary, click **Layout** to choose how your content should be compartmentalized.

Click **Background Colors** to set the colors of your letter, header, email body, and footer. You can also upload images to use as content background.

Enter the email subject and what name should appear in the From field.

**NOTE:** The From email address is set as [info@cvent.com](mailto:info@cvent.com) and cannot be changed.

If you have set up any [reply-to email addresses](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/Setting-Up-Reply-To-Email-Addresses) in your hotel or organization profile, you can use the Reply-To Email drop-down to specify who should receive any replies to this campaign.

Use the available formatting tools at the top of the email body to customize text and [add images](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/How-do-I-add-images-to-Passkey-emails). You can also use [email tags](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/Adding-Tags-to-a-Passkey-Campaign-or-Acknowledgement-Email) to populate specific information based on who the recipient is.

To see how the new campaign email will look like to its recipients, click **Send test message** and enter your email address, then click **Send**.

**NOTE:** Any information included in a test message is generic and not based on your Passkey event or account data.

Click **Save** when you're done.

**7 Enable the new campaign email.** Switch the toggle next to the newly created campaign to **ON** to enable it. Once it is enabled, the email will be sent at the date and time set in its Timing section.

If you enable a campaign at the library level, this will make the email available for selection in Passkey bundles and future events.

Editing a Campaign Email

**1 Access your event campaigns.** Begin by selecting your Passkey event. From the Event navigation, click **Communications**, then **Group Campaigns & Acknowledgements**. The available campaign emails are listed in the Group Campaigns section.

**Or, access the campaigns in your account library.** If you want to edit campaign emails to be used in bundles or future events, hover over Manage in the top navigation, then click **Hotel Setup** or **Organizer Setup**. From the left-hand navigation, click **Libraries**, then **Communications**.

**NOTE:** Changes you make at the library level will not affect existing events.

**2 Edit an existing campaign email.** On the Communications page, click the name of an existing campaign email to preview it. To edit its settings and content, click the ![Edit](https://support.cvent.com/servlet/rtaImage?eid=ka0Ui000000I7O1&feoid=00N2G00000CxUw1&refid=0EM1N000000wDwb) icon to its right.

Make the necessary edits to its timing, recipients, and content, then click **Save**.

**NOTE:** In a hotel library, if a campaign email displays the ![Corporate Standard Template](https://support.cvent.com/servlet/rtaImage?eid=ka0Ui000000I7O1&feoid=00N2G00000CxUw1&refid=0EM2G000001Z0zr) icon, it cannot be edited or deleted as it was created by a parent organization such as a chain or brand.

Removing a Campaign Email

**1 Access your event campaigns.** Begin by selecting your Passkey event. From the left-hand navigation, click **Communications**, then **Group Campaigns & Acknowledgements**. The available campaign emails are listed in the Group Campaigns section.

**Or, access the campaigns in your account library.** If you want to manage campaign emails to be used in bundles or future events, hover over Manage in the top navigation, then click **Hotel Setup** or **Organizer Setup**. From the left-hand navigation, click **Libraries**, then **Communications**.

**NOTE:** Changes you make at the library level will not affect existing events.

**2 Remove a campaign email.** On the Communications page, locate the campaign email you want to delete and click the ![Delete](https://support.cvent.com/servlet/rtaImage?eid=ka0Ui000000I7O1&feoid=00N2G00000CxUw1&refid=0EM2G000001Z0x2) icon to its right, then click **Yes** to confirm.

If you delete a campaign email at the library level, this will not be removed from any existing events it has been added to.

**NOTE:** In a hotel library, if a campaign email displays the ![Corporate Standard Template](https://support.cvent.com/servlet/rtaImage?eid=ka0Ui000000I7O1&feoid=00N2G00000CxUw1&refid=0EM2G000001Z0zr) icon, it cannot be edited or deleted as it was created by a parent organization such as a chain or brand.
