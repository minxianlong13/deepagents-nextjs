# Managing SmartAlerts in Passkey

SmartAlerts are Passkey notifications that can be scheduled or triggered to send to specific recipients. There are two types of SmartAlerts you can create:

-   **Alerts** \- Notifications are triggered when certain inventory conditions or event milestones are met, such as an event block reaching a specific capacity percentage, or an instant booking being created. These conditions can be customized for each event.
-   **Updates** \- Notifications that contain critical event information, such as block, pickup, and reservation details. These can be scheduled to send at regular intervals such as daily, weekly, or monthly.

[Watch this video](https://videos.cvent.com/watch/6ux5hBvSJ9TMLgJWpWg7Ee), or follow the steps below to learn how to [create](#PK_SmartAlert_Create), [edit](#PK_SmartAlert_Edit), [copy](#PK_SmartAlert_Copy), or [remove](#PK_SmartAlert_Del) SmartAlerts both at the event and library levels.

Creating SmartAlerts

SmartAlerts can be created by both hotel users and citywide organizers. Hotel users can add or create SmartAlerts in [Hotel Direct events](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/Passkey-Glossary#H) as well as [Citywide events](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/Passkey-Glossary#C) their hotel participates in. SmartAlerts added by a hotel user to a Citywide event are not visible to the event organizer or to the other hotels participating in the event.

**1 Access your event's SmartAlerts page.** Begin by selecting your Passkey event. From the Event navigation, click **Communications**, then **SmartAlerts**.

**Or, access the SmartAlerts page at the library level.** To manage SmartAlerts for future events, begin by hovering over Manage in the top navigation, then click **Hotel Setup** or **Organizer Setup**.

From the left-hand navigation, click **Libraries**, then **SmartAlerts**.

**NOTE:** Changes you make at the library level do not affect existing events.

**2 Add a SmartAlert.** Begin by clicking **Add a new SmartAlert**.

For library-level SmartAlerts, decide whether you need an Alert or an Update, then click **Add**.

For event-level SmartAlerts, you can select one from your hotel or organization library, then click **Add**. Once you add the SmartAlert, you can [edit its details](#Edit), if desired. Any SmartAlerts you add from the library will be automatically enabled.

If you don't have SmartAlerts defined in your library, or you don't want to use them, click **New Alert** or **New Update** instead.

**3 Set up the SmartAlert.** Name your SmartAlert, then choose the type you need from the Alert Type or Update Type dropdown. To help you decide which type you need, click **View a Sample** to see what each alert would look like.

Set the SmartAlert parameters and frequency. _These will vary depending on the type you selected_. For example, an event cutoff alert will be sent only once, whereas an inventory rule alert can be sent as often as every hour.

Where applicable, select the date when the SmartAlert should start being sent. This can be the reservation open or close date, the event cutoff, or a specific date before or after the reservation open or close date.

Where applicable, select the date when the SmartAlert should stop being sent. This can be the event cutoff, the reservation close date, the event end date, or a specific date before or after the event cutoff or the reservation close date.

Choose the SmartAlert recipients by clicking their names in the Add Recipient list. You can use the **Add all** icon to select all the Passkey users in your account as recipients.

To include recipients that are not Passkey users, add their email addresses in the "Enter Additional Recipients" box.

If you are creating a SmartAlert at the event level, you can include the event users and assigned planners as recipients.

In the Reply-to Email field, enter the email address you want recipients to use if they want to respond to the SmartAlert message.

**NOTE:** The Reply-to Email address is not the same as the one that appears in the From field, which is always set to [info@cvent.com](mailto:info@cvent.com).

Create a personalized SmartAlert message if necessary, then click **Save**.

**4 Enable the SmartAlert.** Toggle the newly created SmartAlert to **ON**. Once you enable a SmartAlert in a Passkey event, the SmartAlert will be sent to the designated recipients either based on the schedule you set up or when a specific event or inventory milestone is met.

You can track your SmartAlerts by [running the Campigns and SmartAlerts report](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/Which-Passkey-report-should-I-run).

Editing SmartAlerts

**1 Access your event's SmartAlerts page.** Begin by selecting your Passkey event. From the Event navigation, click **Communications**, then **SmartAlerts**.

**Or, access the SmartAlerts page at the library level.** To manage SmartAlerts for future events, begin by hovering over Manage in the top navigation, then click **Hotel Setup** or **Organizer Setup**.

From the left-hand navigation, click **Libraries**, then **SmartAlerts**.

**NOTE:** Changes you make at the library level do not affect existing events.

**2 Edit a SmartAlert.** Click the **Menu** icon next to the SmartAlert you want to modify, then select **Edit**.

**NOTE:** In a hotel library, if a campaign email displays a Corporate Standard Template icon next to its name, it cannot be edited or deleted, as it was created by a parent organization, such as a chain or brand.

Edit the SmartAlert parameters, frequency, or recipients. 

**NOTE:** You cannot change the type of a SmartAlert while editing it.

Click **Save**.

Copying a SmartAlert

**1 Access your event's SmartAlerts page.** Begin by selecting your Passkey event. From the Event navigation, click **Communications**, then **SmartAlerts**.

**Or, access the SmartAlerts page at the library level.** To manage SmartAlerts for future events, begin by hovering over Manage in the top navigation, then click **Hotel Setup** or **Organizer Setup**.

From the left-hand navigation, click **Libraries**, then **SmartAlerts**.

**NOTE:** Changes you make at the library level do not affect existing events.

**2 Copy a SmartAlert.** Click the **Menu** icon next to the SmartAlert you want to copy, then select **Copy**.

Give the new SmartAlert a name, then click **Copy**.

Make the necessary edits to its parameters, frequency, or recipients, then click **Save**.

Removing a SmartAlert

**1 Access your event's SmartAlerts page.** Begin by selecting your Passkey event. From the Event navigation, click **Communications**, then **SmartAlerts**.

**Or, access the SmartAlerts page at the library level.** To manage SmartAlerts for future events, begin by hovering over Manage in the top navigation, then click **Hotel Setup** or **Organizer Setup**.

From the left-hand navigation, click **Libraries**, then **SmartAlerts**.

**NOTE:** Changes you make at the library level do not affect existing events.

**2 Remove a SmartAlert.** Click the **Menu** icon next to the SmartAlert you want to remove, then select **Delete**. Click **Yes** to confirm.

**NOTE:** In a hotel library, if a campaign email displays the Corporate Standard Template icon next to its name, it cannot be edited or deleted, as it was created by a parent organization, such as a chain or brand.
