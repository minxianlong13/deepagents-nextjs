# Why isn't Passkey connecting or syncing with my Cvent event?

FAQEvent ManagementPasskeyIntegrations

If you've [enabled the integration between your registration event and Passkey](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/The-Complete-Guide-to-Integrating-Events-with-Passkey) but the data is still not syncing, or if you're getting a "Could not connect to Passkey. Please try again later" error when trying to connect your registration to a Passkey event, ensure the RegLink connection between Passkey and Cvent is on.

Begin by selecting your Passkey event. Or, if the event was created by a licensed organization or venue instead, ask them to access it for you. From the left-hand navigation, click **Details**, then **API Access**. Switch the RegLink toggle next to Cvent Events to **ON**.

If Cvent Events is not listed, click **ADD A PROVIDER**, then search for and select "Cvent Events." Switch the RegLink toggle next to it to **ON**. In the Success pop-up message that appears, click **Yes**.

Click **Save**. Once you've fixed the issue, only future bookings will be synced. Existing bookings have to be canceled and added again.
