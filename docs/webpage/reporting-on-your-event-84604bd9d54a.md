# Reporting on Your Event

Topic HubEvent Management

Event reports are grouped into categories to help you locate the one you need. To access them, begin by selecting your event. From the Event navigation, hover over **Reports**, then click **Reports** again. Depending on what features were enabled in the event, the following categories display in the Report Templates tab:  

-   **Agenda Item** - Displays data based on your event's agenda items and sessions.
-   **Attendee** - Shows everything related to your attendees and registrations, such as abandoned registrations, cancellation details, substitutions, and answer details. 
-   **Attendee Hub** - Tracks everything related to [Attendee Hub](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/Welcome-to-Attendee-Hub) such as login data, discussion details, polling and usage.
-   **Email** - Shows data related to the emails you sent within your event.
-   **Exhibitor** - Shows exhibitor details, statuses, and leads when using [exhibitor management](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/Getting-Started-with-Exhibitor-Management).
-   **Feedback & Assessment** \- Tracks data collected from your event [assessment](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/Using-Assessments-in-Event).
-   **Feedback** - Tracks data collected from your [session feedback surveys](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/Setting-Up-the-Event-Feedback-Survey#session).
-   **Finance** - Assists in keeping track of your event's financial transactions.
-   **Planning** \- Assists in keeping track of your [budgeting](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/PSR-Budgeting-101) information.
-   **Onsite** - Tracks data collected onsite using [OnArrival](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/Meet-Our-OnArrival-Check-In-App).
-   **RFID Support** - Tracks data collected onsite using radio frequency identification (RFID).
-   **Speaker** - Helps track speaker information through the [Speaker Resource Center](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/Getting-Started-with-Speaker-Resource-Center) as well as a list of speakers with their associated sessions.
-   **Travel** - Focuses on [travel data](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/A-Tour-of-Travel) such as air actuals, air requests, hotel requests, and group flights, along with travel questions.

You can collapse or expand a category by clicking its name.
