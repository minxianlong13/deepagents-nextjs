# Key Dates and Times in Passkey Events

A Passkey event is governed by a series of key dates that determine [when reservations can be made](#PK_Details_ResDates), when is the last day a Citywide organizer has exclusive control over the event inventory, what a [hotel's inventory nights](#PK_Hotel_inv_dates) are, or when a hotel participating in a Citywide event can [start viewing and managing reservations](#PK_Hotel_inv_dates).

Event Dates

The Event Start and End dates indicate the first and last date, respectively, when guests can attend sessions, exhibitions, keynotes, and other activities included in the event. The event dates display on the [booking website landing page](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/The-Complete-Passkey-Booking-Site-Experience#PK_Web_Landing), both in the banner at the top of the page and highlighted blue in the check-in and checkout calendars. You can modify these dates by going to _Details > Dates_.

Reservation Dates

The following event reservation dates can be configured under _Details > Dates_:

**NOTE:** For each reservation date except the cutoff, you can also set a specific time. For example, selecting "End of Day" from the time dropdown means reservations will open or close at 23:59:59 on the selected date.

-   **General Open** - indicates the first date and time when someone can make a reservation for the event.
-   **General Close** - Indicates the last date and time when someone can make a reservation for the event.
-   **Cutoff date** - only shows up in Citywide events, and represents the last day when the event organizer has exclusive control over the event inventory. By default, the organizer's exclusive control over the inventory ends on the selected date, at 11:59 PM. After this date, a hotel participating in the Citywide event can make any adjustments to their event inventory, such as updating dates, rates, the current block, or inventory settings. The cutoff date is unique across the event, no matter how many hotels are participating. You can use also use the event cutoff date as a milestone to implement sales strategies in your Citywide event, such as [releasing allocated inventory to Free-Sell](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/Setting-Up-Release-to-Free-Sell-Rules-for-Sub-Blocks), [washing inventory](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/Scheduling-Inventory-Tasks-in-Passkey), or [selling primary inventory](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/Scheduling-Inventory-Tasks-in-Passkey#SellPrimary). When used as a milestone for scheduled tasks, the default time for the event cutoff is 12:00 AM on the selected date.
-   **Web Open** - indicates the first date and time when someone can make a reservation through the [event booking website](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/The-Complete-Passkey-Booking-Site-Experience). 
-   **Web Close** - indicates the last date and time when someone can make a reservation through the [event booking website](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/The-Complete-Passkey-Booking-Site-Experience).
-   **Call Center Open** - indicates the first date and time when someone can make a reservation through the [call center reservation form](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/Understanding-the-Call-Center-Reservation-Form-in-Passkey). 
-   **Call Center Close** - indicates the last date and time when someone can make a reservation through the [call center reservation form](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/Understanding-the-Call-Center-Reservation-Form-in-Passkey). If Call Center users should have the ability to modify or cancel reservations after the Call Center Close date, check the box next to "Allow Call Center changes after Close date". 

Hotel Inventory Dates

To access the Hotel Inventory dates, begin by selecting your Passkey event. On the Event Overview page, click the name of the hotel to access the inventory. Hover over Inventory Tasks in the top-right, then click **Manage Dates**.

**NOTE:** If your hotel is participating in a Citywide event, you can only access and update the hotel inventory dates after the event cutoff date has passed.

The following inventory dates can be set: 

-   **Reservation Access date** - only shows up in Citywide events. Effective from the start of the selected date, a participating hotel, either integrated or non-integrated, gains access to reservation data. This means they can start viewing and managing reservations for their hotel in [RezHub](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/Using-Passkey-RezHUB), the [Multi-Room Booking tool](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/Using-the-Multi-Room-Booking-Tool), and the [call center form](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/Using-the-Call-Center-Form-to-Modify-or-Cancel-a-Passkey-Reservation), they start receiving [hotel reservation notification emails](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/Updating-a-Hotel-s-Reservation-Contact-Email-in-Passkey), and they can start [running specific reports](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/Which-Passkey-report-should-I-run) such as the Room List or Delegate report. For non-integrated hotels, this is also the date when they can start [downloading room lists](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/Using-the-RoomList-Download-Feature) and manually importing reservations into their property management or reservation management system. By default, the reservation access date for a participating hotel is set as the day after the [event cutoff](#PK_Cutoff_date). For example, if the event cutoff date is set to May 5th, the default reservation access date will be May 6th. You can, however, manually change the reservation access date for a participating hotel. If the hotel has a [GroupLink integration set up](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/Setting-Up-GroupLink-for-a-Passkey-Event) for the event, changing the reservation access date will offset the reservation start transfer date in the GroupLink setup by the same number of days. [Reference this article](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/Why-are-reservations-missing-in-RezHUB) to learn more about how long reservation data is available in RezHub once the reservation access date is reached.

**NOTE:** Changing the reservation access date from a past date to a future one will not remove any reservation data that has already been published to RezHub, nor will it re-process reservations that have already been transferred.

-   **Nights in the Contracted Room Block** - shows the date range defined in the contract agreed between the event organizer and the participating hotel. These can be different from the actual event date range. The end date for the contracted room block must fall on or before the inventory end date.
-   **Inventory Dates** - shows the date range, including shoulder nights, where rooms are being sold for the event. You cannot decrease the number of inventory nights if reservations have already been made for the dates you want to remove. If you increase the number of inventory dates, you will be prompted to decide whether you wish to apply rates from the adjacent nights to the new nights. You can apply the rates at both the room and sub-block levels, or at the room level only.
-   **Hotel Close date** - indicates the last day when the hotel accepts new reservations for the event from any booking channel (e.g. booking website, room lists, call center forms, or the multi-room booking tool). The Hotel Close time defaults to end of day. After this date, the hotel status will display as Shutoff under [_Advanced Options > Hotels_](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/Managing-Advanced-Options-for-Hotels-in-Passkey-Events). You can still update existing reservations after this date, but you cannot create new ones.
-   **Hotel Cutoff date** - only shows up in Hotel Direct events, and is effective from 12:00 AM on the selected date. You can use this date as a milestone to implement specific selling strategies such as [releasing allocated inventory to Free-Sell](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/Setting-Up-Release-to-Free-Sell-Rules-for-Sub-Blocks), [washing inventory](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/Scheduling-Inventory-Tasks-in-Passkey), or selling primary inventory. The hotel cutoff date should fall before the hotel close date.
-   **Disable Commerce** - only shows up in Citywide events that use a [Merchant Account](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/The-Complete-Guide-to-the-Merchant-Account-Processing-Feature-in-Passkey). Effective from the start of the selected date, any deposit, processing, and cancellation fees are no longer charged or processed for reservations booked at a specific hotel participating in your Citywide event. This allows the hotel to directly manage charges and refunds on their end.
