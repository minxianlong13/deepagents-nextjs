# If a Passkey booking is canceled, will the registration get canceled too?

FAQEvent ManagementPasskey

If your Cvent event is [integrated with Passkey](https://support.cvent.com/s/communityarticle/Setting-Up-the-Passkey-Integration-in-Registration), canceling an attendee’s Passkey booking will not automatically cancel their registration in Cvent. The attendee will need to access their registration to modify or cancel it.

Does your attendee need to make a new booking after canceling the original one? Instruct them to go back into their registration and use the **Select Hotel** option on the Confirmation page.
